The Kitchen Computer, 1969

Honeywell's H316 — marketed to housewives as a recipe-filing machine. A $10,000 computer positioned as a domestic appliance, dressed in an apron.

Computer History Museum ✦ Wikipedia: Honeywell 316 ✦

if she can only cook..., San Jose Museum of Art, April 2026

Artist reenacts the original advertisement — inhabiting the role assigned to women by technology marketing, and exposing its assumptions through live performance.

Details ✦ Motherboards at SJ MoA ✦

If she can only cook as well as Honeywell can compute.

Her souffles are supreme, her meal planning a challenge? She's what the Honeywell people had in mind when they devised our Kitchen Computer. She'll learn to program it with a cross-reference to her favorite recipes by N-M's own Helen Corbitt. Then by simply pushing a few buttons obtain a complete menu organized around the entrée. And if she pales at reckoning her lunch tabs, she can program it to balance the family checkbook. 84A 10,600.00 complete with two week programming course.
